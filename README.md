# ProofPost AI 🚀
### Know Before You Post.

ProofPost AI is an **AI-powered Micro SaaS platform** that helps student creators evaluate their content *before* publishing — giving them a detailed **Creator Confidence Report** in seconds, instead of leaving them guessing.

> **One Upload → One AI Report → One Confident Publishing Decision**



## 🧩 The Problem

Student creators spend **3–6 hours** creating a single piece of content, but before hitting publish they're stuck wondering:

- Is this original, or too similar to something already out there?
- Is my hook strong enough in the first few seconds?
- Is my thumbnail actually attractive?
- Should I post now, or improve it first?

Existing AI tools only help **generate** content — captions, edits, thumbnails, scheduling. None of them answer the creator's real question:

**"Is my content actually ready to publish?"**



## 💡 The Solution

ProofPost AI doesn't generate content — it **validates** it. Creators upload their video/reel, thumbnail, caption, script, and hashtags, and receive an instant, detailed breakdown:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| 🎯 Originality Score | How unique your content is |
| 🪝 Hook Strength Score | Whether your first few seconds grab attention |
| 🖼️ Thumbnail Quality Score | If your thumbnail is scroll-stopping |
| ✍️ Caption Quality Score | How well your caption performs |
| 📈 Trend Saturation Analysis | How oversaturated the trend/topic is |
| 📖 Readability | How easy your content is to consume |
| ✅ Overall Confidence Score | The final verdict |

**Final Recommendation:** `Ready to Post` ✅ or `Improve Before Posting` 🛠️
